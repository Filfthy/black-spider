The cards

  • 60 cards: red, blue, purple and green, each numbered 1 to 11, and black 1 to 11 plus five more black 7s.
  • The six black 7s are the Black Spiders.
  • The small number under a card's number is what it scores if you take it in a trick.

Rules

  • 3 to 5 players, and every card is dealt: 20 each with 3 players, 15 with 4, 12 with 5.
  • Passing
    Before each hand everyone passes three cards: to the left, then the right, and so on. With 4 players it goes left, right, then across.
  • Playing
    Whoever holds the red 1 leads it to the first trick. If you have the colour led, you must follow it: play a card of that colour, or a card of the same number instead. If you have none of the colour led, play anything.
  • The highest card of the colour led wins the trick. Other colours never win, so a black card only wins when black was led. If black is led and several Black Spiders are played, the first one wins. The winner leads the next trick.
  • Scoring
    Points are bad. Black 1 to 6 score 1 each, black 8 to 11 score 2 to 5, and each Black Spider scores 10. The coloured 7s are −5 each: good to take. Every hand adds up to 60.
  • The sweep
    Take all 16 black cards and you score 0 for the hand, and everyone else scores 60.
  • The game ends when someone reaches 200 (3 players), 150 (4) or 120 (5). Lowest total wins.

Tips

  • Pass your Black Spiders and high black cards if you can, or keep low black cards to duck under them.
  • Any 7 can be played on a 7. Lead a 7 and someone may drop a Black Spider on you.
  • When you can't follow, it's your chance to get rid of a Black Spider.
  • When it's your turn your name glows, and cards you can't play are dimmed.
